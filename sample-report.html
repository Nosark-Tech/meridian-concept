PHOTO 2.4 · REAR BOUNDARY

REF 2.4 · SITE

Retaining wall leanMAJOR

WHERE
Rear boundary, timber sleeper wall.
WHAT I FOUND
Approximately 100mm lean over a 4.2m run. Posts soft at ground line in two locations. No weep holes.
WHY IT MATTERS
Progressive failure. A collapse risks the neighbour's fence line and the rear yard levels.
WHAT NEXT
Structural engineer or licensed retaining-wall contractor before settlement. Budget for full rebuild of that bay.
PHOTO 3.6 · LIVING CEILING

REF 3.6 · ROOF / INTERIOR

Active roof leakMAJOR

WHERE
Living-room ceiling, south-east corner; corresponding roof valley above.
WHAT I FOUND
Water staining and blistered paint. Valley flashing lifted. Underlay damp to touch after overnight rain.
WHY IT MATTERS
Active water entry. Left alone it spreads into the roof void and plaster.
WHAT NEXT
Licensed roof plumber. Temporary tarp if rain is forecast before repair.

What I could not inspect

Named plainly, because the gaps matter as much as the findings.

  • Inside wall cavities and under fixed floor coverings.
  • Second-storey roof void — hatch sealed by the ceiling lining.
  • Areas behind dense vegetation along the west boundary.
  • Anything under locked garden sheds.

How it runs

Booked in a phone call. Reported by tomorrow.

Four steps, and you're only involved in two of them.

01

Today · 5 minutes

Tell us the address

We confirm which inspector covers it, when they're free, and what the inspection costs before you commit to anything.

02

Inspection day · 2 to 3 hours

Your inspector attends

Roof, roof void, subfloor, interior, exterior, garage, drainage and retaining. You're welcome to come along and ask questions while he works.

03

Within 24 hours

The report lands

Photographed, located, rated major or minor, plain English, with the summary on page one and the access limits named on the last.

04

Same day as the report

Your inspector rings you

He walks you through what he found, what it's likely to cost, and which two items actually matter before your cooling-off period ends.

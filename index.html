How it runs

Clear from the first call to the final question.

Four deliberate steps. You only need to be present for the ones that matter.

01

Today · 5 minutes

Tell us the address

We confirm which inspector covers it, when they are free, and what the inspection costs before you commit.

02

Inspection day · 2 to 3 hours

Your inspector attends

Roof, void, subfloor, interior, exterior, garage, drainage and retaining. You are welcome to ask questions while they work.

03

Within 24 hours

The report lands

Photographed, located, rated major or minor, in plain English, with the summary on page one.

04

Same day as the report

Your inspector rings you

They walk you through what was found, what it could cost, and which items matter before cooling-off ends.
